## Direction contract
THESIS: A graphic opening menu becomes a case-file portfolio. The user's four supplied Persona UI screenshots supersede the earlier conservative hero: overlapping lettering, diagonal planes, sharp selection silhouettes, and a dedicated equipment selector must be structural, not decorative additions to a standard grid.

OWN-WORLD: Signal red #ee163d, ink #080808, paper #faf9f5, restrained cyan #7de0ef. Barlow Condensed plus licensed Alfa Slab One create mixed cut-paper lettering; upright Barlow carries descriptions. Original JU type geometry replaces any character art. Irregular backplates, halftone fields, thick offset borders, and rotated type plates recur across screens.

STORY: Meet a student developer, select current work, inspect previous repositories, discover tools, and connect. All development statuses and planned features remain explicit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One red/black diagonal scene, an upper-left layered Joshua/Urrea lockup, a center-right selectable text menu over a giant outlined JU, and lower-left introduction/CTA. The menu overlaps the visual field rather than occupying a separate column. Mobile gives the name and menu their own vertical reading order; paragraphs remain clear of graphic overlaps.

FORM: Reference-led refinement of the user's pinned world; no concept alternatives requested. Case-file selection and equipment categories share one accessible tab selector. Arrow keys/Home/End retain focus. Main navigation gives a quick diagonal sweep without delaying fragment navigation. No perpetual motion, forced loading, audio, or game assets. Reduced motion removes sweeps/wipes while preserving selection feedback.
